The scene listing says what exists, not what it looks like. When a step is about appearance — a layout that may be broken, a button that may be covered, a sprite in the wrong state, text that may be unreadable — call `capture_screen` and judge from the picture. Screenshots are limited, so spend them on the steps where looking decides the verdict.

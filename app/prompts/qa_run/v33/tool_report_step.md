Record the verdict for one scenario step, once per step, right after observing its result. For a verifying step `passed` is whether the expected result occurred; otherwise whether you carried the action out. `message` cites what you saw, `thought` how you got there. `used_knowledge_ids` lists only entries that changed your verdict, as printed to you; leave it empty otherwise.
